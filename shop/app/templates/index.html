How it works

  1. Pick a key and pay

    Checkout runs on Stripe's page, so your card details never touch our site.

  2. Copy your key

    It shows on the confirmation page as soon as the payment goes through.

  3. Claim it in Discord

    Run /claim_key and paste the key. You get the Tracker Access role, and we DM you when your time is up.

Questions

When does my time start?

When you run /claim_key, not when you pay. You can buy now and claim later.

Can I buy more than one key?

Yes. If you claim a second key while you still have access, its time is added to what you have left.

What happens when my time is up?

We remove the Tracker Access role and send you a DM. The role comes off either way: if your DMs from the server are closed, the message can't be delivered.

I lost my key.

Open the confirmation page again from the link Stripe sent you back to. If that's gone, open a support ticket in our Discord and have the email you paid with ready. Lost keys can't be refunded.

Can I get a refund?

No. All sales are final, including lost keys. If you have a problem with your purchase, open a support ticket in our Discord and our staff will look into it.
