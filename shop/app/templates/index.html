How it works

  1. Pick a key and pay on Stripe's checkout page.
  2. Copy your key from the confirmation page.
  3. In our Discord, run /claim_key and paste the key. You get the Tracker Access role, and we send you a DM when your time is up.

Questions

When does my time start?

When you run /claim_key, not when you pay. You can buy now and claim later.

Can I buy more than one key?

Yes. If you claim a second key while you still have access, its time is added to what you have left.

I lost my key.

Open the confirmation page again from the link Stripe sent you back to. If that's gone, ask the staff in our Discord and have the email you paid with ready.
